Research

ClutterBench: A Benchmark for Evaluating Vision-Language-Action Models in Cluttered Environments

Yuxuan Li*, Juneyoung Yoon*, Vaidehi Dalmia*, Yiyang Ling, Ue-Hwan Kim, Daniel Seita (* equal contribution)

NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Vision Language Models: Challenges of Real-World Deployment (VLM4RWD)

project page · openreview

A benchmark for testing VLA policies on manipulation in cluttered scenes, spanning four kinds of clutter, evaluated across seven models in simulation and three on a real robot. Beyond success rate, it measures how much the policy disturbs objects it was not asked to move.

in progress

Ongoing work on VLA policies for manipulation in progress

A follow-up to ClutterBench that looks at physical clutter: scenes where the environment itself gets in the way of the arm, not just the objects on the table. Details to come once the work is ready to share.
